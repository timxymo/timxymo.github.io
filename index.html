Philosophy & Technique

How Each Piece Is Made

Committed to honest materials, structural integrity, and hand-finished surfaces.

01

Lumber Selection & Grain Harmony

Every board is chosen by hand for color consistency, figure, and grain direction from domestic hardwoods like walnut, white oak, cherry, and maple.

02

Engineered Joinery

From blind mortise-and-tenons and sliding dovetails to bent laminations and coopering, joinery is planned with seasonal expansion and long-term durability in mind.

03

Tactile Surface Preparation

Surfaces are hand-planed, card-scraped, and progressively sanded to 320+ grit to bring out depth and clarity in the natural grain.

04

Natural Hardwax & Oil Finishes

Protected with plant-based hardwax oils and food-safe finishes that penetrate deep into the fibers, providing satin luster and water resistance.

About the Maker

Bridging Analytical Precision with Handcraft

Hi, I’m Tim Mo. What started as simple DIY projects has grown into a focused journey in fine woodworking, custom cabinetry, and furniture design.

With a background in computational mathematics and statistics, I bring deep appreciation for geometry, precision tolerances, and structural design into the wood shop. Whether building coopered seating, bent lamination trays, or custom-dimensioned tables, my goal is to build pieces that marry utility with timeless aesthetics.

Key Materials American Walnut, White Oak, Cherry, Hard Maple
Techniques Mortise & Tenon, Dovetails, Bent Lamination, Turning
Finishes Rubio Monocoat, Odie's Oil, Food-Safe Beeswax
Location Northern Virginia / DC Metro
